SoC chart — Empty layout

Fleet managers and equipment operators need real-time battery and charging visibility to prevent downtime and boost fleet productivity

SoC chart screenshot

The Challenge
When does this situation occur?

Fleet managers and operators face key challenges in battery monitoring...

  • Extended data gaps:
    Trucks that haven't transmitted data for more than 24 hours create blank, uninformative charts.
  • Weekend/off-hours:
    Equipment downtime appears as "missing" data Monday mornings.
  • 30+ day inactivity:
    Completely empty charts across all time ranges.
  • Inconsistent visuals:
    Different chart styles per time view create confusion about data vs. equipment status.
User Research Insights

Based on user testing and stakeholder interviews, we identified key pain points:

"I can't tell if the truck is broken or just not sending data"

Uncertainty about equipment status versus data transmission issues.

"The charts look completely different between time ranges, it's confusing"

Visual inconsistency created cognitive load.

"When I check on Monday, everything looks empty, but I don't know if that's normal"

Lack of context about expected operational patterns.

The Solution
In‑chart message:

No information has been received for the past 24 hours.

Placement:

Message displayed directly inside the chart area.

Range awareness:

Copy adapts to the selected view: Last: 24h, 7 days and 30 days.

Visual hierarchy:

Chart frame, axes, and legend remain visible while the message communicates the data gap.

SoC chart - Short intervals

Fleet managers need clear pattern visibility in battery data to make strategic maintenance and operational decisions effectively.
The Challenge
When does this situation occur?

Fleet managers struggled with chart readability due to excessive visual noise from brief operational activities:

  • Micro-operations:
    Equipment operations lasting only seconds or minutes created numerous tiny data points.
  • Scale-dependent readability:
    In 24-hour views, short intervals were interpretable due to fewer total data points, but became overwhelming in 7-day and 30-day views.
  • Pattern interference:
    Brief operational spikes made it difficult to identify meaningful usage trends and battery performance patterns.
User Research Insights
Based on user feedback and observation sessions, we identified critical issues:
"These tiny spikes make it hard to see the real usage patterns"

Visual clutter impaired trend analysis.

"I can see what's happening in the 24-hour view, but the weekly view is just noise"

Scale-dependent readability problems.

"I need to understand the big picture, not every single minute of operation"

Strategic versus operational information needs.

  • Refine the algorithm to filter out short intervals (from the development side).
